# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical tradeoffs without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing personal information.
- Accept feedback and help keep discussions focused and accessible.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and deliberate
intimidation are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the
project.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, limit participation,
or exclude participants for serious or repeated violations. Moderation should
be proportionate and account for context.

Report concerns privately through a contact method that a repository maintainer
has explicitly made available on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive
personal details in public issues. If no private contact is available, ask a
maintainer for a private reporting method without describing the incident
publicly. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and disclose information
only as necessary to address the concern.
